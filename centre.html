Enter the centre

Rest your awareness at the point of light and notice whether feeling and understanding hold together. Pinch, scroll or press Go in: inside the point is the same figure again, without end. Offered to be tried, not as a finding.

What the figure shows

The base of the triangle is the felt perspective, I1; the apex is the understanding perspective, I2; the point of light at the centre is I3, the witness's eye, the one point every level shares. The circle is π, a mark and not a name. Each circle inside is exactly half the one before, so the figure is the same at every scale: a drawing of the author's reading (Interpretive), not a measurement.
