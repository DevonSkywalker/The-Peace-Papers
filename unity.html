An interactive model of group dynamics

Polycentric unity, from paper VII.

Seven circles, each centred on its neighbours' rims, so that the edge of each runs through the centre of the next and every corner of a triangle is a neighbour's centre. They breathe. Each circle is a group with its triangle, the group's ego, and its centre, the witness; and each is also a member of the circle around it and a group of the circles within it, so the one model gives every scale. Tap one to knock it out of place: the others hold it, and it comes back.

An image, not evidence (Interpretive). Peace is arrived at at equilibrium, but in reality everything is moving, so it is held in dynamic equilibrium: the balance is kept each moment, by every circle, or it is lost.
